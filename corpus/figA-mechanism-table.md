# Figure A — Mechanism table

Sourced only from the A2 draft's Literature Review (`A2 Draft v1 — 2 Sep 2026.md`, ¶ on
Dourish & Bellotti / Whitson / Stray, and the synthesis paragraph) and the SAFE-TO-WRITE
lists in `verify/dourish1992.md`, `verify/whitson2018.md` and `verify/reserve.md` (section B,
Stray). No claim below goes beyond that wording.

| Mechanism | The mechanism as its source states it | What it predicts an automated report can repair | What that implies for the method |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Structural** (Dourish & Bellotti, 1992) | "The individual supplies information at a cost repaid to the group rather than to the individual; recipients receive only what the sender deems appropriate; and delivery is controlled by the sender, who cannot predict what will be needed or when" (§3.1). Against this, D&B set a taxonomy of informational, role-restrictive and shared-feedback mechanisms, the last "collected passively as a by-product of the work itself" (§3–4, §6). | Because the failure is the cost/control asymmetry in the *act of reporting*, not a gap in what exists, passive collection of shared feedback "as a by-product of the work itself" repairs it: reading the artefact instead of asking removes the sender's cost, relevance-judgement and timing control from the loop. | "Reading artefacts instead of asking becomes a mechanism claim, not a convenience" — it is the peer-reviewed ancestry of an aggregation layer that computes state from the artefact rather than asking the developer to report it. D&B's own scope is narrow (four groups, one 90-minute session, participants in separate rooms, §4.1–4.3), so extending it to asynchronous, week-long work is the method's own claim to defend, not one D&B make. |
| **Opacity, plus blame on the tool** (Whitson, 2018) | Developers used the term "voodoo" for tool behaviour whose cause-and-effect they could not predict from their own input — the tool was opaque to its own users. That ambiguity was attributed to the tool rather than to a teammate, letting the group move on without assigning blame or exposing anyone's lack of expertise. Underpinning both, engines and art tools operated as boundary objects that disciplines worked through without shared expertise. | If developers cannot predict what the tool did, extraction "can surface what changed but not why" — an automated report built from the artefact record can repair the *visibility* half (what changed) but not the *causal/explanatory* half (why it happened), because that explanation was never available to the developer either. | Asking practitioners what their tools fail to tell them under-reports by construction, since the gap is opacity rather than withholding. A report generator therefore "enters a negotiation, not a vacuum": it supplies a rival mechanism to structural under-reporting, one the synthesis has to adjudicate against rather than simply add to. |
| **Audience mismatch** (Stray, Sjøberg & Dybå, 2016) | A stand-up turned status report "serves the manager, not the team" — the same reporting artefact is read differently depending on who it is produced for. | An automated report faces the same fork: producing an accurate account of activity does not by itself fix whose needs that account serves, so the mechanism predicts that a report generator can repair *accuracy* without repairing *audience fit* — the two are separate failures. | The report's design has to be built for its stated audience (the non-technical producer) rather than assumed to generalise from a developer-facing account; audience mismatch is the reason the project treats "who reads this" as a design variable rather than an afterthought. |

Note: the third row is deliberately the thinnest of the three. The draft's Literature
Review states the audience-mismatch finding itself ("a stand-up turned status report
serves the manager, not the team") but does not spell out a repair-prediction or a
method implication for Stray et al. (2016) the way it does for Dourish & Bellotti and
Whitson — the two right-hand cells above stay close to that one stated finding rather
than adding a claim the draft does not make.
