# Risk assessment: COMP3180 A3 user study

Draft, 14 Oct 2026. Style follows the National Statement (2023) Ch 2.1: judge the likelihood and the severity of harm or discomfort, then say what reduces it. Risk categories used: minimal, low (discomfort only), greater than low (harm). Source for the categories: National Statement 2023, Ch 2.1, Figure 1 (https://www.swslhd.health.nsw.gov.au/Ethics/content/pdf/National-Statement-Ethical-Conduct-Human-Research-2023.pdf, retrieved 14 Oct 2026). Ratings are my judgement and need the reviewer's agreement. Rating scale: Low / Medium / High.

## Why not "greater than low"
No foreseeable risk of harm: no sensitive topic, no health or personal data collected beyond a year-level label and role, no payment, no deception beyond "one item cannot be answered" (which is disclosed in the briefing), no vulnerable group, and no consequence for any mark. Risk of distress is limited to mild discomfort. This is my reading; the reviewing body decides.

## Things the reviewer might raise
- Whether classmates of the researcher in the same unit are a "dependent or unequal relationship" (Ch 4.3). I have treated it as one to be safe.
- Whether public GitHub usernames count as personal information (probably yes, but public). I have treated them as personal information.
- Whether the "unanswerable item" counts as deception. It is disclosed, so I treat it as test design, not deception. [CONFIRM]
