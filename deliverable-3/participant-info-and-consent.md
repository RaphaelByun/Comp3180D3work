## Information sheet (participant keeps this page)

**Study:** How well can a producer understand a game team's week from an automatic one-page report?
**Researcher:** Raphael Byun, COMP3180 student, Macquarie University. Contact: [student email].
**Unit convenor:** [name, email].

**What is this about?** I have built a tool, ChangeLens, that reads a Unity game project's version history and writes a one-page summary of what changed each week. I want to find out what a reader can and cannot learn from that page. The project is my COMP3180 final project.

**What would I do?** One session of about 45 minutes (35 minutes if you are not asked to do the last part). You will:
1. read a short scenario in which you play a game producer (or, if you work as a producer, hear a one-line description of the setting);
2. read two weekly reports from an open-source game (SS3D) and answer six short questions about each;
3. fill in a one-page sheet about which records could answer ten common producer questions;
4. for some participants: talk aloud while you jot down a short stand-up update, then explain three of your answers on the sheet.

This is not a test of you. There are no right answers on the sheet in step 3.

**Do I have to take part?** No. Taking part is voluntary. Saying no, or stopping, has no effect on your marks, your team, or your standing in any unit. You can stop at any time during the session without giving a reason. You can also ask for your sheets and notes to be removed from the study until **[date: proposed 1 November 2026, before analysis starts]** by emailing me with your participant code.

**What is recorded?** Your answer sheets, your sheet of ticks, your stand-up notes (if you do that part), and my written notes of what you say and do, with times. **No audio or video is recorded.** I will not record anything beyond written notes unless you agree to it separately, in writing, in advance.

**How is my information protected?** Every sheet carries a code (for example R03), never your name. The only list linking names to codes is kept on a separate sheet, apart from the data, and is used only if you ask to withdraw. It will be destroyed on **[date]**. Paper sheets are kept **[where]** and typed-up data are stored **[where]** until **[date / retention period required by the university]**.

**Where will results appear?** In my COMP3180 report, video and supporting files, which are marked by unit staff and may be shown in a portfolio. Results are reported by code or as group totals. Short quotes of what you said may be used, by code only. Nothing that identifies you will be published.

**Risks and benefits.** No risks are expected beyond the time taken. You may find it interesting to see a producer tool from the inside. There is no payment.

**Questions or concerns?** Contact me at [student email], or the unit convenor at [email]. [If ethics approval applies: the approval number and the university's ethics contact go here.]
