I record scores and take notes by hand, not audio or video [Raphael: confirm before sending]. Your name is not kept with your answers and is not in anything I publish; you get a code instead.

> **When and where:** Weeks 11 and 12 (19 Oct to 1 Nov), on campus or on a call. Reply here, or find me at the prac, and I'll offer a time.
>
> Thank you,
> Raphael
