# Outside-viewer feedback form (the A2 success measure)

A2: **"two outside viewers can state its purpose and reader."**

Use this with two people who are not in your project, did not take part in the study, and have not been told what ChangeLens does. Ideally not COMP3180 students, because they know the topic. One viewer may be a producer or game-industry person; one may be a general viewer.

## How to run it (5 minutes each)

1. Before the video, tell them it is voluntary, they can stop at any time, and only a viewer code (V1, V2) and their answers are kept, with no name. This is the same basis as the reader study: no ethics application, so voluntary and de-identified (`deliverable-3/README.md`, ethics decision of 17 Oct; `deliverable-3/ethics/report-ethics-points.md`). If they say no, ask someone else.
2. Then give them the rough cut with no introduction. Say only: "Please watch this video. It is 3 to 4 minutes. I will ask five short questions afterwards."
3. Do not pause, explain or hint while they watch.
4. Hand them the form below (or read it out and write their words down exactly). Ask all five questions before telling them anything. Only questions 1 and 2 are marked. Question 3 is a note on whether they saw the two versions, and questions 4 and 5 are notes for fixing the cut.
5. Mark pass or fail with the key. Then you may discuss.
6. If either viewer fails, fix the video and test with a new viewer (the same person has seen it already).

## Form (print or copy)

Viewer code: V1 / V2 (no names)  Date: ______  Watched on: phone / laptop / TV
Relationship to games: none / play games / study or work in games

1. In one or two sentences, what is the thing in this video for? (Its purpose.)

   ______________________________________________________________

2. Who is it for? (Its reader or user.)

   ______________________________________________________________

3. What did the video show about the two versions of the report, if anything?

   ______________________________________________________________

4. What was the most confusing moment? (Give a rough time if you can.)

   ______________________________________________________________

5. Anything you wanted to know that the video did not tell you?

   ______________________________________________________________

## Marking key (for Raphael only, do not show)

| Question | Pass if the viewer says something equivalent to | Fail if |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Purpose | It turns a week of changes in a game project into a short report, so someone can see what happened without reading code or relying on people to write it up | Only "a Unity tool", "a game", "a testing app", or a feature list with no sense of why |
| 2 Reader | A game producer or project manager, or someone who does not code but needs to know what the team did | "Developers", "players", "everyone", or no answer |
| 3 Both conditions | Notices there are two versions and that one names things (optional, supports the "both conditions" measure) | Not scored, but if neither viewer notices, beat 4 and 5 are too fast |

Questions 3, 4 and 5 are not scored. Use their answers to choose what to fix.

Measure met when both viewers pass Q1 and Q2. Record the outcome as a count: "2 of 2 viewers stated both" (or "1 of 2, then fixed and retested with V3").
