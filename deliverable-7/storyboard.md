## Rules

- Show both conditions on screen long enough to read one row from each (at least 6 seconds each).
- Real tool, real report. No mock-ups. Everything shown must be reproducible from the repo.
- February Version B: the airlock rows are only in the February week (the July week has none), so keep February for beats 4 and 5. Frame the zoom on the airlock rows, below the "25 scenes and prefabs changed" line. Do not linger on the pepperspray or Wet Floor Sign rows. Version A is unchanged. To show exactly the committed page instead, scroll the July week (`study/week2-2021-07-19`), where the window and the committed pages match, and use February for the airlock zoom only.
- Result slot: use real numbers only. If analysis is not finished by recording time, record everything else and re-record beat 6 alone.
- Never show a reserve-week page (`study/reserve/`) in any shot before SE2 is scored (plan section 3). Recording before then uses the February and July study weeks only.
- Show no session footage or quotes, and say nothing about the evaluation design (21 Oct rule). Do not show SS3D contributors' email addresses (the export logs hold them); crop any commit list to the files.
- Third-party material: SS3D is open source (name it and cite it); cite any music or images on the end card or in the report's References.
- Captions on; audio clean; screen at 1080p; large fonts (Unity at 125% or more) so text reads on a phone.
- Check Echo 360 upload guidance and the 1-hour technical grace period; upload at least a day before 8 Nov.
